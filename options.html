How this works & legal notice

Kept in full, out of the way of the controls.

  • You must already be logged in to your CB portal in this browser — the extension reuses that session and never sees or stores your password.
  • When you visit a site, the extension guesses a brand name from its domain and matches it against the local catalogue.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere except requests to the portal origin you approve here.
Legal notice

This is a private, unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, operated by, endorsed by, or reviewed by corporate benefits Germany GmbH. All brand names, product names and logos belong to their respective owners.

The offer catalogue is deliberately downloaded only once a week – staggered rather than in one burst – and all matching afterwards happens locally in your browser. This is done explicitly to keep load on corporate benefits' servers as low as possible: instead of a search request on every page you visit, only a handful of requests are made per week.

Discount figures come from the locally cached catalogue and may be out of date. Only the offer page on the portal itself is authoritative. No warranty, no liability.

The offers and discount codes are subject to your portal's terms of use and are confidential. They are intended for eligible employees only and must not be shared with third parties or published.
